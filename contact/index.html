What to include so we can help quickly

A question about NestPlan

Tell us which iPhone model and iOS version you use, whether you have the Free or PRO version, and what you expected to happen compared with what happened. Please do not email us account balances or other private financial details. The NestPlan support page answers common questions about restoring purchases, where your data is stored and how Monte Carlo simulation works.

A question about WeatherBolt

Include your iPhone model, iOS version and whether the problem involves the radar, forecasts, lightning alerts, tides or the Watch app. For alert problems, mention whether notifications are enabled in your phone settings. The WeatherBolt support page covers purchases and data sources, and our alerts guide lists things to check.

A problem with a football calendar

Tell us which calendar you subscribed to, which calendar app and device you use, and what looks wrong, such as a missing game or a wrong time. A screenshot helps. Many issues are caused by how calendar apps refresh or by time zone settings, so please look at our calendar troubleshooting guide first. If you still need help, we would like to hear from you.

A question about Sprig

Sprig has its own website with contact details at heysprig.com.

A question about this website or its guides

If you have found a mistake in a guide, a broken link or a page that does not display well, please tell us the page address and what you saw. Corrections are always welcome. If you have a topic you would like us to cover, send it along.

Privacy requests

To ask about information we hold, or to request correction or deletion, email us with the subject line "Privacy request". Our Privacy Policy explains your options, including how to control advertising cookies.

Ads on this site

This site is supported by advertising provided by Google AdSense. If you see an ad that seems inappropriate or misleading, send us the page address and a short description, and we will look into it.

What we cannot do by email

  • We cannot give personal financial, tax or legal advice. Our guides are general education only.
  • We cannot give weather or safety advice for a specific situation. Please use official NOAA, National Weather Service and Coast Guard sources.
  • We cannot change the contents of someone else's website or app.
  • We cannot refund App Store purchases, which are handled by Apple. Our support pages explain how to request a refund.
