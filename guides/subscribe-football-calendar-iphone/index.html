Add a College Football or NFL Calendar to Your iPhone

Calendar guides · By CoreForge LLC · Published November 5, 2026

Kickoff times are easy to lose track of when they live on a website you have to remember to open. A subscribed calendar fixes that, because the games show up next to your dentist appointment and your kid's practice schedule. This guide walks through adding one of the College Football Calendars or NFL Calendars to an iPhone or iPad.

What you are actually adding

Both calendar sites publish standard .ics feeds. That is the same open format Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and Outlook all understand. When you subscribe, your iPhone does not download a one time list of games. It stores a link and checks that link again from time to time, so changes to kickoff times, networks and final scores arrive on their own.

Each page offers a few kinds of calendar. The college football site has All FBS, a Top 25 calendar, a list of conferences and a list of teams. The NFL site has All NFL, the AFC and NFC and their divisions, and a list of all 32 teams. Pick the narrowest one that still covers what you care about, since a single team calendar is much quieter than the whole league. If you are not sure where to start, the shorter the calendar, the easier it is to live with.

Method 1: the one tap subscribe link

This is the fastest route and works well if you are already on your iPhone.

  1. Open Safari on your iPhone or iPad and go to the calendar site you want.
  2. Find the calendar you want. For All FBS, Top 25 and All NFL, tap the Subscribe link on the card. For a conference, division or team, choose it from the picker and tap Get link first, then use the subscribe link that appears.
  3. iOS asks whether you want to subscribe to the calendar. Confirm, and the Calendar app opens with the new subscription.
  4. Open the Calendar app and tap Calendars at the bottom of the screen. Make sure the new calendar has a check mark next to it.

The subscribe link uses a webcal:// address, which is a signal to your phone that the link belongs in a calendar app rather than a web page. If Safari shows you raw text instead of offering to subscribe, use the manual method below.

Method 2: add it by hand in Settings

The manual route is useful if you copied the calendar link from a message, if the one tap method did nothing, or if you want to be sure which account the subscription lives in.

  1. On the calendar site, tap Get link or press and hold the subscribe link and copy the address. A copied address that begins with https:// or webcal:// will both work in the next step.
  2. Open Settings. Go to Calendar and then Accounts. On newer versions of iOS, the Calendar settings sit inside an Apps section of Settings, so scroll for that if you do not see Calendar listed directly.
  3. Tap Add Account, then Other, then Add Subscribed Calendar.
  4. Paste the address into the Server field and tap Next.
  5. Tap Save when the details appear.

Menu names shift a little between iOS versions, so treat the labels above as a guide rather than a script. The path always has the same shape: an accounts area, an option to add a subscribed calendar, and a place to paste a link.

Avoid the Download .ics button for ongoing updates

Each calendar card also has a Download .ics button. That one gives you a file. If you open the file on an iPhone, you will be offered a chance to add every event to a calendar once. Those events are copies, and nothing keeps them current. If a kickoff moves or a score comes in, your copy will not know. Use the file only if you deliberately want a frozen snapshot. For everything else, subscribe.

If you already imported a file and now see duplicate games after subscribing, delete the imported events or the calendar that holds them, and keep the subscription.

Tidy it up after subscribing

A subscribed calendar is read only, which means you cannot drag games around or edit them. You can, however, change how it looks on your own device.

  • Rename it. In the Calendar app, tap Calendars, then the small information button next to the football calendar. Give it a short name such as "Alabama" or "Chiefs".
  • Pick a color. A color you do not use for anything else makes game days stand out in the month view.
  • Hide it in the off season. Uncheck the calendar in the Calendars list and its events disappear from your views without removing the subscription.

The feeds describe the matchup in the event title. Once a game is final, the title and description carry the final score. NFL events also note the TV network in the description. The feeds do not set reminder alerts of their own, so if you want a nudge before kickoff, look at the alert options your Calendar app offers for the calendar, or add your own reminder for the games that matter most to you.

How often will it update

The score and schedule data behind the calendars refreshes every night, and the feed itself asks calendar apps to check back about once a day. On an iPhone, the exact timing of the check also depends on your device settings, such as how it fetches new data, whether Low Power Mode is on, and whether the Calendar app has been opened recently. In practice, expect changes to show up within a day, not within minutes. If a game moved this morning and your phone still shows the old time, wait for the next refresh, and if it still looks wrong the next day, the troubleshooting guide covers what to check.

Using more than one device

A subscription that you add in Settings on an iPhone is stored on that phone. If you also use an iPad or a Mac, the safest approach is to add the subscription on each device you want it on, or to add it once on a Mac and store it in iCloud so it follows your Apple Account. The Mac guide explains the iCloud option. Family members need to subscribe on their own devices, since a subscription belongs to one person's account and settings.

Removing the calendar

When the season ends, or you simply want fewer calendars, there are two choices. Hiding it, as described above, keeps the subscription ready for next year. To remove it completely, open the Calendar app, tap Calendars, tap the information button beside the football calendar, and scroll down to the delete or unsubscribe option. You can also go to the Calendar accounts area in Settings, open the subscribed calendars list, choose the football calendar and delete it. Removing a subscription never affects your other calendars or any events you created yourself.

Quick checklist

  • Pick the smallest calendar that covers your needs.
  • Use the Subscribe link, or paste the copied link into Settings.
  • Skip the Download .ics button unless you want a one time copy.
  • Rename and recolor the calendar so it is easy to spot.
  • Be patient: updates arrive on a daily rhythm.
