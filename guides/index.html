Practical how to articles written by the team behind NestPlan, WeatherBolt and the football calendar tools, plus plain language money basics.

18 guides in four topics

Every guide here is meant to be useful on its own, whether or not you use our apps. The calendar guides give step by step instructions for the apps people already have. The NestPlan and WeatherBolt guides explain what those apps do and the ideas behind them, using only what the apps actually offer. The money articles cover everyday budgeting and saving habits with simple worked examples. All of it is general education and not personal advice, which our Terms of Use explain in more detail.
