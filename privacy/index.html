Privacy Policy

CoreForge LLC · Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit coreforgeapps.com, how we and our advertising and analytics partners use it, and the choices you have. We have tried to write it in plain language.

The short version. We do not ask you to create an account on this website. We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, and we show ads through Google AdSense. Google and other third party vendors use cookies for those purposes. You can control them using the links in this policy.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This website is operated by CoreForge LLC, 30 N Gould St #N, Sheridan, WY 82801, US. You can reach us at team@coreforgeapps.com.

This policy covers the pages of coreforgeapps.com, including the guides and the information pages about our apps. Some other things have their own policies:

  • The College Football Calendars and NFL Calendars websites have their own privacy pages.
  • The NestPlan privacy policy and the WeatherBolt privacy policy describe how those iPhone apps handle data.
  • Sprig has its own privacy policy on heysprig.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. There is no sign up form, comment system or account system on this site.

Information collected automatically

When you visit, your browser sends information that is needed to deliver the page, and our hosting and analytics tools may record details such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, the pages you view, the page that referred you, the date and time of your visit and your approximate location based on your IP address. This information is used in aggregate to understand which pages are useful and to keep the site working.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files that a website or a service it uses stores in your browser. We do not set cookies of our own to track you across the web. The cookies on this site come from the third party services described below: Google Analytics and Google AdSense.

You can control cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers let you block cookies, delete them or ask for a warning before one is stored. Blocking all cookies may affect how some websites work, but this site is built to remain readable without them.

4. Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service provided by Google, to measure how visitors use the site, such as which pages are most visited and roughly where visitors come from. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect usage information, and Google processes that information for us. We use the reports to improve our content. We do not use analytics to identify you personally.

You can prevent your data from being used by Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics opt out browser add on. You can also learn more about how Google handles this data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertising on some pages. This helps pay for the site and lets us keep the guides and tools free to read.

  • Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can also opt out of some third party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices. Opting out does not stop ads from appearing. It means the ads you see may be less relevant to you.

Depending on where you are, you may be shown a consent message that lets you choose whether advertising and measurement cookies are used. Your choice there controls how those cookies are used on your visit. Google's own policies describe how it processes the data it collects through its advertising products.

We do not control the cookies set by Google or other advertising partners. Advertisers on the platform may also use their own technologies. Please read the privacy policies of those parties for details.

6. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain and protect the website
  • To understand how the site is used and to improve the content
  • To show advertising that helps fund the site
  • To respond to messages sent to us
  • To meet legal obligations and protect our rights

7. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information for money. Information may be handled by the following kinds of parties:

  • Analytics and advertising providers, such as Google, as described above.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers, which deliver this website to your browser and may keep standard server logs.
  • Authorities and advisers, when we are required by law, or when needed to protect our rights, our users or the public.
  • A successor, if CoreForge LLC is ever involved in a sale or reorganization, in which case this policy would continue to apply to information already collected unless you are told otherwise.

8. Your choices

  • Manage or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Opt out of personalized ads through Google's Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics with the browser add on linked above.
  • Email us to ask what information we hold from our own correspondence with you, or to ask us to correct or delete it.

9. Privacy rights by region

California and other US states. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information a business has collected about you, to ask for it to be deleted or corrected, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. Some uses of advertising cookies may fall under those definitions. You can opt out through the advertising controls above. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. You may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent that you have given. Where we rely on consent, such as for advertising cookies, you can withdraw it at any time through the consent message, your browser, or Google's tools. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email team@coreforgeapps.com. We may need to confirm your identity before we act on a request, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

10. Retention

We keep emails that you send us for as long as is reasonably needed to deal with your request and to keep our records. Analytics data is kept according to the retention settings we choose in Google Analytics. Data held by advertising and hosting providers is kept under their own policies.

11. Children

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Links to other sites

Our pages link to other websites, including our calendar sites, third party services and the App Store. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other sites, and we encourage you to read their policies.

13. Protecting information

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we handle, and the site is delivered over an encrypted connection. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly safe, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection.
